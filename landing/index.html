Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who train regularly and want a clean way to plan, track, and review their week.

Week & month views

See your entire week at a glance. Swipe between weeks, tap any day to add or review sessions. Month grid gives you the big picture.

Activity heatmap

A 26-week heatmap shows your training rhythm. Color-coded by sport, dimmed for planned sessions. Your consistency, visualized.

20+ sport types

Run, swim, cycle, hike, yoga, padel, climb, and more. Each type has its own color. Add custom sports from 40+ Strava categories.

Weekly goals

Set a target — 3 sessions a week, 5, 7. A progress ring tracks your done count against your goal. Simple accountability.

Calendar sync

Sync your planned sessions to your phone calendar automatically. Edit in Kadence, see it everywhere.

Strava integration

Import your entire Strava history — years of runs, rides, and swims light up the heatmap instantly. New Strava activities auto-mark your planned sessions as done.

Plan

Your week, laid out.

Every day has a card. Tap to add a session — pick the sport, set a time, add notes. Swipe to navigate between weeks. Double-tap to mark done.

Review

See your patterns.

The stats screen turns months of sessions into a clear picture — streaks, heatmaps, type breakdowns. Know where you're consistent and where you're slipping.

Customize

Make it yours.

Dark or light mode. Pick your accent color from 7 palettes. Customize type colors. Choose your week start day. Set your language.
