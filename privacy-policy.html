Privacy Policy

Kadence — Last updated: May 2026

What Kadence does

Kadence is a mobile app for planning and tracking weekly sports activities. It stores your workout sessions locally on your device and optionally syncs them to the cloud when you sign in.

Data we collect

Kadence collects the minimum data needed to function:

  • Account information — If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from the provider. This is used to identify your account and display your name in the app.
  • Activity data — The workout sessions you create (name, type, date, time, duration, notes, and completion status). This data is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, synced to our cloud database so you can access it from multiple devices.
  • Calendar data — If you enable calendar sync, Kadence creates, updates, and deletes calendar events corresponding to your planned sessions. Kadence does not read or access any other calendar events.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or cookies.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

Where your data is stored

Activity data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. If you sign in, your data is also stored in a cloud database hosted by Supabase (servers located in the EU/US). Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS. Access to your cloud data is protected by Row Level Security — only you can read or modify your own activities.

Authentication

Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth using Google and Apple as identity providers. Kadence never sees or stores your password. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you use the app. You can delete individual activities at any time from within the app. If you sign out, your cloud data remains stored so you can access it again later. To permanently delete all your data and your account, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Kadence is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Material changes will be communicated through the app.
